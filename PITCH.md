# Flux — Canonical Pitch

> The founder-approved articulation of what Flux is. Use this as the source of
> truth for marketing copy, decks, app-store text, and AI prompts (NotebookLM etc.).

## Core idea

Instead of being a note-taking app, **Flux is a course memory**.

Imagine you're taking Calculus.

Throughout the semester you:

- Record every lecture.
- Upload every PDF.
- Upload homework solutions.
- Save YouTube videos your professor recommends.
- Write your own notes.
- Add textbook excerpts.

Flux connects all of that into **one knowledge base**.

Then you can ask:

- *"Explain the Chain Rule the same way Professor Smith did."*
- *"Show me where integration by parts was mentioned."*
- *"Generate a practice exam using everything we've covered so far."*

Instead of answering from general knowledge alone, Flux answers from **your
actual course materials** and **cites where the information came from**.

## One-liner

**"The AI that went to every one of your classes."**

## Usage notes

- Public copy should say "show me **where** X was mentioned" rather than
  "**every time** X was mentioned" — retrieval surfaces the most relevant
  passages, not a guaranteed exhaustive list.
- Every claim above is shipped and true as of July 2026 (record, files,
  YouTube, notes, cited Q&A, quiz/practice generation from course memory).
